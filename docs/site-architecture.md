# Agents DX Marketing Website Architecture

## Experience intent

Agents DX will be presented as a confident AI automation partner for agencies and client-acquisition teams. The visual language prioritizes sharp contrast, disciplined grid structure, deliberate whitespace, and Coral action cues rather than mimicking the referenced site's soft grid and green accent approach. The concept is an original **command-centre editorial** design: an intelligent product surface supported by concise evidence, operational clarity, and direct conversion paths.

| Layer | Decision | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand palette | Coral `#FF6D5A`, Deep Slate `#384D5B`, Black `#000000`, White `#FFFFFF`, Cloud `#F8FAFC`, Night `#0F172A` | Implements the supplied high-contrast, premium AI SaaS identity. |
| Typography | Zen Dots wordmark; Inter for headings, body copy, and interface | Keeps the brand mark distinctive without sacrificing reading quality. |
| Layout | 12-column desktop grid, stacked narrow-screen flow, oversized editorial headings | Communicates modern operational sophistication across responsive contexts. |
| Motion | Brief transform/opacity transitions, signal-line animation, reduced-motion fallback | Adds energy while preserving usability and performance. |
| Conversion | Persistent header CTA, paired hero CTAs, page-end decision panels | Maintains direct paths to "Get Started" and "Book a Demo". |

## Public route system

| Route | Page responsibility | Content blocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | Introduce the platform and communicate outcome | Hero, feature signal rail, product surface, workflow, social-proof-ready section, CTA. |
| `/features` | Explain the AI automation operating model | Capabilities grid, workflow sequence, automation detail, implementation CTA. |
| `/pricing` | Help a buyer select an engagement level | Monthly/annual view toggle, tier cards, comparison table, social-proof-ready section, CTA. |
| `/about` | Establish the company story and values | Mission, narrative, values, team placeholder, CTA. |
| `/contact` | Capture a sales inquiry | Client-side validated inquiry form and a calendar embed placeholder. |
| `/resources` | Organize original educational material | Curated card grid and category metadata. |
| `/resources/:slug` | Provide an individual resource page | Article content, related-resource links, CTA. |
| `/faq` | Resolve common evaluation questions | Category-led accessible accordion. |
| `/privacy-policy`, `/terms-of-service` | Preserve the required legal navigation endpoints | Clearly marked legal-review placeholders. |

## Content governance

Product claims, prices, integrations, client names, client logos, testimonials, star ratings, and performance statistics will not be fabricated. The social-proof component is engineered as a reusable, data-driven surface and appears in a transparent **evidence-ready** state until Agents DX supplies authorised source material. Pricing is designed for tier comparison without presenting unapproved commercial terms. The wordmark is represented with the approved Zen Dots treatment until an official logo file is supplied.
