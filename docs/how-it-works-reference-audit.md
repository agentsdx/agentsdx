# Public How It Works Reference Audit

The public `dmchamp.com/#how` section was reviewed on 18 August 2026 as a structural reference only. Its code, asset treatment, proprietary copy, revenue claims, testimonials, agency-reseller narrative, and white-label positioning will not be reused.

| Public structural pattern observed | Original agents DX interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| A sequential setup flow | A company-focused **Orient → Design → Deploy → Improve** operating loop. |
| Product configuration visuals | An original workforce command canvas that illustrates context, operating role, and human review rather than copied UI. |
| Capability cards after the setup steps | A practical assurance section covering support, sales, booking, continuity, and governed handoffs. |
| Activation CTA | A direct end-user/company CTA to plan a workforce deployment or book a demo. |

The agents DX page will not mention clients, agency plans, reselling, white-label, billing another party, custom domains for clients, or markup economics. It will speak directly to teams and companies deploying AI workforces for their own Support, Sales, and Booking operations.
