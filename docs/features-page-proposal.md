# Proposed Agents DX Features Page

## Reference Review Scope

The public [`dmchamp.com/features`](https://dmchamp.com/features/) page was reviewed **only for its information architecture**. The original copy, visual assets, product claims, and agency or white-label positioning will not be reused. The useful structural pattern is a broad capability directory that moves from an orienting statement to capability groups, an availability comparison, answers to common questions, and a final decision-oriented call to action.

## Original Agents DX Page Structure for Approval

| Section | Original agents DX purpose | Proposed content direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Hero: Your AI Workforce, Clearly Defined** | Establish the platform as an operating system for practical AI roles. | A clear headline, short product explanation, and a compact workforce command-canvas visual. CTA buttons: **Get Started** and **Book a Demo**. |
| **2. The Workforce at a Glance** | Give visitors a fast mental model of the platform. | Three role cards: **Support that responds**, **Sales that follows through**, and **Booking that moves people forward**. |
| **3. Intelligence Layer** | Explain how agents work responsibly rather than list generic AI claims. | Context, approved knowledge, human handoff, and continual improvement—shown as a four-step operating loop. |
| **4. Conversation & Signal Capture** | Show how the workforce recognizes and routes meaningful demand. | Intent detection, response preparation, escalation rules, and lead-context capture, framed as operational outcomes rather than channel-specific imitation. |
| **5. Workflows That Convert** | Make the practical outcomes concrete. | Feature cards for qualification paths, follow-up rhythms, appointment readiness, and team handoffs. Each card includes a short “what changes” result statement. |
| **6. Control, Oversight & Integration** | Build trust with decision-makers. | Role controls, shared visibility, integration readiness, governed handoffs, and transparent operating signals. No reseller, white-label, or agency tooling. |
| **7. Platform Availability** | Help visitors understand what comes with each offering. | A concise comparison between **agents DX Platform** and **agents DX + Dedicated Manager**, aligned to the existing pricing page without inventing feature availability. |
| **8. Feature Questions** | Remove common friction before a demo. | Five to six concise FAQs focused on deployment, human oversight, integrations, support, and getting started. |
| **9. Final CTA: Put the Workforce to Work** | Convert high-intent visitors. | A dark closing panel leading visitors to **Get Started** or **Book a Demo**. |

## Visual Direction

The page would preserve the established agents DX system: deep-slate command surfaces, coral signals, off-white spacing, and Inter-led hierarchy. It would be more editorial than a generic card grid, alternating between structured product canvases, concise capability matrices, and large outcome-led typography. Every section will remain mobile-first and keyboard-accessible.

## Explicit Exclusions

The proposed page will not mention or imply white-labeling, reselling, client sub-accounts, agency plans, credit reselling, or agency workflows. It will not duplicate the reference site’s language, screenshots, graphics, individual product-feature copy, or channel-specific claims.
